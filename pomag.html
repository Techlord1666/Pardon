Leadership & Accountability

POMAG Is Not an Independent Ministry

POMAG operates under the Church Council Board (CCB) and the Senior Pastor. Every outreach activity requires approval, strategic clearance, and accountability.

"Obey them that have the rule over you..."

Hebrews 13:17

The success of POMAG depends largely on effective oversight and support from the CCB.

Our Outreach Process

Eight steps, followed in order, every time

01

Prayer

Every outreach begins with prayer.

02

Approval

Obtain the required approval.

03

Location Assessment

Assess the target territory/location.

04

Mobilization

Prepare and mobilize the outreach team.

05

Evangelism

Carry out the evangelism/outreach.

06

Follow-Up

Follow up with people reached.

07

Retention

Help converts become established.

08

Reporting

Document and report the outcome.

No outreach shall be conducted outside these principles.
