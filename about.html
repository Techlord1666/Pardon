About Our Ministry

Winning the Lost at All Cost

Understand God’s Love

You know God loves you, but do you really understand what that means? Our heart is for people to know and experience the depth of His love and be made complete in the fullness of life that comes from Him.

Make an Impact

As followers of Christ, we are to care for our neighbors, feed the hungry, defend the powerless, and love the unlovable. Then we will truly make a difference in our community and the world.

Discover God’s Word

The Bible is God’s message for us so we can know Him better. We are not only hearers of God’s Word, but we take what we learn and let it transform our lives, grow in our faith and draw us closer to Him.

Receive God’s Blessing

God’s best for you comes when you seek Him with all your heart. As a church, we want to see people draw near to God so we can discover His will for us and receive His blessings, and in turn bless others.

Our Beliefs

One God

There is one God, who is infinitely perfect, existing eternally in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Jesus Christ is the true God and the true man...

Deuteronomy 6:4, Matthew 5:48, Matthew 28:19, Philippians 2:6–11

Divinity of the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is a divine person, sent to indwell, guide, teach, empower the believer, and convince the world of sin.

John 14:15–18, John 16:13; Acts 1:8

Authority of Scriptures

The Old and New Testaments, inerrant as originally given, were verbally inspired by God and are a complete revelation of His will.

2 Peter 1:20–21, 2 Timothy 3:15–16

Repentance

Commitment to turn away from sin in every area of our lives and to follow Christ to receive His redemption.

Acts 2:21, 3:19, 1 John 1:9

Salvation

Provided through Jesus Christ for all men; those who repent and believe are born again. We are saved by grace through faith.

Titus 3:4–7, Ephesians 2:8-9, Romans 10:9-10

Baptism of the Holy Spirit

The will of God that each believer should be filled with the Holy Spirit and sanctified wholly for holy living.

1 Thessalonians 5:23, Acts 1:8, Romans 6:1–14

Marriage

God created marriage. It is a covenant made between a man and a woman that makes them one.

Gen 2: 22-24; Matt 19:4-6

God's Will for Provision

It is the Father's will for believers to become whole, healthy and successful in all areas of life: Spiritual, Mental, Emotional, Physical, and Financial.

Physical: Isaiah 53:4-5
Mental: 2 Timothy 1:7
Financial: Malachi 3:10
Spiritual: John 3:3

Resurrection

Jesus was physically resurrected. Both the saved and the lost will be resurrected to their eternal destinations.

Luke 24:39, John 20:26-28

The Church Body

The Church consists of all those who believe on the Lord Jesus Christ. The local church is a body of believers joined together for worship, edification, and witness.

Ephesians 1:22–23, Matthew 28:19–20, Acts 2:41–47
