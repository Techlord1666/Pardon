Years of Faith

The Journey of Grace

The Founding

In a small living room in the heart of the city, twelve souls gathered with a shared vision of a church that felt like home—a sanctuary from the bustling industrial world.

Expanding the Walls

By the 1960s, our community had grown to encompass diverse voices, leading to the construction of our current stone sanctuary, designed to mirror the natural world.

A Digital Horizon

Today, Sanctuary transcends physical boundaries, connecting thousands across the globe through a shared digital experience rooted in ancient wisdom.
