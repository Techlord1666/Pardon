Our Heart & Mission

Winning the lost at all cost through the power of God's Word.

Understand God’s Love

You know God loves you, but do you really understand what that means? Our heart is for people to know and experience the depth of His love and be made complete in the fullness of life that comes from Him.

Make an Impact

As followers of Christ, we are to care for our neighbors, feed the hungry, defend the powerless, and love the unlovable. Then we will truly make a difference in our community and the world.

Discover God’s Word

The Bible is God’s message for us so we can know Him better. We are not only hearers of God’s Word, but we take what we learn and let it transform our lives, grow in our faith and draw us closer to Him.

Receive God’s Blessing

God’s best for you comes when you seek Him with all your heart. As a church, we want to see people draw near to God so we can discover His will for us and receive His blessings, and in turn bless others.

Statement of Faith

Our Beliefs

One God

There is one God, who is infinitely perfect, existing eternally in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Jesus Christ is the true God and the true man.

He was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. He died upon the cross, the Just for the unjust, as a substitutionary sacrifice, and all who believe in Him are justified on the ground of His shed blood.

He arose from the dead according to the Scriptures. He is now at the right hand of Majesty on high as our great High Priest. He will come again to establish His kingdom, righteousness and peace.
